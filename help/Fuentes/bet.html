How to place a bet

To place a bet, you need to previously have chips added to your account, and next, you'll need to select a chip and a box to select a bet

Bear in mind that you can only bet one chip each time, but, once, you confirm the bet, you can bet for more boxes but just with a single chip for each bet.



Notice that once you select a box and a chip, both panels remain disabled until you confirm/cancel the actual bet

Once you confirm the bet, is remains displayed in the list down of the right side
